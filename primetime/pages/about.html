Her story

Hi, I'm Sarha Nicholson — the owner behind Prime Time Pressure Washing. I started this business to bring an honest, personal touch to a service that a lot of people put off because they're not sure what it costs or who to trust.

I show up myself, walk the property with you, and give you a clear, fair price before any work begins. Every job — big or small — gets the same care and attention to detail.

Based right here in Pittsburgh, I take pride in treating every home and driveway like it's my own, and in making the whole process — from quote to payment — as easy as possible.
