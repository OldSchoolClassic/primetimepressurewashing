Common questions

Before you request a quote

The questions we hear most from homeowners considering pressure washing for the first time.

Will pressure washing damage my siding or driveway?

No — we match the right pressure and technique to each surface. Siding gets a gentler soft wash to lift mold and algae without stripping paint or forcing water behind panels, while concrete can safely handle higher pressure to lift deep stains.

How much does it actually cost?

It depends on the size and condition of what you're cleaning — see the pricing above for set rates on trash cans, vehicles, and sidewalks, and per-square-foot pricing for siding and concrete. Driveways are quoted individually. Either way, you'll always get a clear price before we start, never a surprise after.

How long does a typical job take?

Most single-service jobs (a driveway, a stretch of siding, a sidewalk) take between 1–3 hours depending on size and condition. We'll give you a time estimate along with your quote.

Do I need to be home while you work?

Not necessarily — as long as we have access to the area being cleaned and a water source, many customers simply go about their day. We'll coordinate details when we schedule your job.

What if I'm not happy with the results?

Then we're not done. Every job comes with our satisfaction guarantee — if something isn't right, tell us and we'll make it right before we consider the job complete.
