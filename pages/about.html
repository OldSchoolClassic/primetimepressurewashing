Our story

Built on honest work and a fair price.

Prime Time Pressure Washing was founded to bring a straightforward, trustworthy option to a service that a lot of homeowners put off — because they aren't sure what it costs or who to trust with their property.

We walk every property, big or small, with the same process: a clear look at what needs cleaning, an honest price before any work begins, and careful, well-executed work when the job starts. No hidden fees, no upsells, no surprises at the end.

As a local Pittsburgh company, we take pride in treating every home, driveway, and business like it's our own — and in making the entire process, from quote to payment, as easy as possible.
